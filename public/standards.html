Permissible error for a load

Accuracy classes

Which class an instrument can claim is fixed by its verification scale interval and its number of intervals. Both limits are checked when an instrument is registered.

Maximum permissible errors

Errors are limited in steps of the verification scale interval. The step boundaries depend on the class; the limits do not. In service every limit is doubled.

Tests and their criteria

Each test's rule is stored with the clause it comes from. The engine reads these; it does not hard‑code them.
