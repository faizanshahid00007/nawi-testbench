What the software does

Everything the Department of Consumer Affairs asked for in problem statement SIH26035, built the way a metrology laboratory works.

Instruments and their specifications

Applicant, manufacturer, model, serial, category, Max, Min, e, d, tare effects, power supply, temperature limits, software identification, load cells and interfaces. The class is checked against Table 3 as you type.

R 76-1 3.2, 3.9.2, 7.1

Laboratory and environmental conditions

Temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, supply voltage and frequency, recorded at the start, during and at the end of testing, each reading checked against the instrument's own limits.

R 76-1 3.9.2, 3.9.3, A.4.1

Every prescribed test, with validation

Seventeen data-entry forms, one per R 76 test, from weighing performance to span stability and the markings checklist. Impossible readings are refused; implausible ones are recorded with a warning that follows them into the report.

R 76-1 3.5 to 3.9, 5.3, 7.1, Annexes A and B

Permissible errors and pass or fail, automatically

The MPE band for every load, the changeover-point correction, the zero-error correction and the criterion for each test are applied the moment a reading is saved. The verdict for the point, the test and the whole evaluation update together.

R 76-1 3.5.1, 3.5.2, A.4.4.3

Report, Word file, PDF and certificate

The standardised report in the R 76-2 layout, as HTML, PDF and an editable Word document, with photographs embedded. A certificate of conformity is issued only when the whole battery passes and an approver has signed.

R 76-2 section 1

Repository, search and roles

Every test is kept, searchable by reference, certificate, applicant, instrument, status, outcome, class and date, with instrument-wise history. Viewers read, engineers record, approvers sign, admins manage users. Every action is in the audit trail.

Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011

Errors finer than the display can show

P = I + ½e − ΔL    Ec = E − E0

A digital indicator only shows whole multiples of e, so subtracting the reading from the nominal load cannot resolve an error smaller than one increment. But in the lowest band the tolerance is 0.5 e. The Recommendation's answer is the changeover point: add weights of one tenth of e until the display steps to the next value, and recover the indication before rounding from how much it took.

The result is then corrected by the zero error determined immediately beforehand, and it is that corrected error, not the raw one, that is compared with the permissible error. Both steps are done for every point where the engineer records an added load, both are shown in the report, and both appear on the live readout before the point is saved.

The battery

Read live from the rule set. Each test knows its clause, its criterion, which instruments it applies to and whether type approval or verification requires it.

Loading the rule set…

From first reading to certificate

A test moves through four states. Each transition is recorded with who did it and when.

Recording

An engineer opens the test against one instrument, one rule set and one purpose, records conditions and observations, attaches photographs.

engineer

Closed

Observations are frozen and the outcome at closing is filed. Closing an incomplete battery has to be done deliberately.

engineer

Approved and signed

An approving officer reviews the report and signs it. The signature is a SHA-256 hash over every observation, verdict and the approval itself.

approver

Certified

If the whole battery passed, a numbered certificate of conformity is issued. Anyone can confirm it at a public verification address.

automatic

The standard is data, not code

Accuracy classes, permitted ranges of n, the band tables, the in-service multiplier, the supply-voltage rules and each test's criterion live in a versioned rule-set file. A new edition of R 76 is a new file. Every report stores the identifier of the rule set it was judged under, so a report from years ago can be recomputed exactly as it was.

Each constant carries the clause it was taken from, checked against R 76-1 Edition 2006 and R 76-2 Edition 2007. The rule set can be inspected, and the permissible error for any load worked out, on the standard page.
