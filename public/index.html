What the software does

Built to the brief of SIH26035, the way a metrology laboratory works.

Instrument register, laboratory conditions, seventeen R 76 test forms with validation, automatic permissible errors and verdicts, a searchable repository with roles, and the report, the Word file, the PDF and the certificate.

The standard is data, not code.

Accuracy classes, MPE bands, supply-voltage rules and every test's criterion live in a versioned rule-set file. A revised R 76 is a new file, and every report pins the rule set it was judged under.

// rulesets/oiml-r76-2006.json
"III": { "bands": [[0, 500, 0.5], [500, 2000, 1.0], [2000, 10000, 1.5]] },
"creep": { "clause": "3.9.4.1 / A.4.11.1", "within30MinE": 0.5, "between15and30E": 0.2 }

Validation with metrological sense.

Impossible readings are refused. Implausible ones are saved with a warning that follows them into the report. A live readout in every form shows the corrected error against ± MPE before you save.

  • ✕Load above Max + T, tare above T, missing time or second reading
  • !Indication not a multiple of d, ΔL above one e, condition outside the instrument's limits

A certificate anyone can verify.

Issued only when the full battery passes and an approver has signed with a SHA-256 over every observation and verdict. The QR code opens a public verification page.

Multi-interval instruments, handled properly.

Up to three partial ranges, each classified on its own. The MPE, the changeover step and the discrimination weight follow the e of the range the load falls in.

The science, made visible

A small error. A meaningful decision.

A digital display only shows whole multiples of e, yet the smallest tolerance is half an interval. The changeover point recovers the reading before rounding.
